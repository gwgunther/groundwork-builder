Novel components (4)
Full-Bleed Photo Hero Card
Large-radius card containing a warm photo. Nav overlays the photo's top edge in white/transparent. Hero text in lower-left quadrant — not centered. Single white-fill pill CTA.
Bento Feature Grid — Photo Center
3-column bento where the center column is a tall photo. 4 flanking white cards each have a circular icon badge (warm fill, stroke icon), heading, and 2-line description. No list rows, no prices.
Portrait Team Grid + Social Badge
3-up white cards: tall warm portrait fills top of card, name + role + circular phone/social icon badge on footer row. Used for doctor + staff introduction.
Metadata Eyebrow Chip
Pill-fill chip carrying schedule or status metadata above a service H1 (e.g. 'New patients welcome', 'Mon · 9 AM'). Adapts the standard category-chip to a trust-signal function. No new component required — token variant only.
Fidelity checks (12)
Hero is a single large-radius card with a full-bleed warm photo. No split-image or text-only hero layout.
Nav overlays the hero photo at the card's top edge. On inner pages, nav is dark-on-cream without a card wrapper.
Single button style only: near-black fill (#1C1614), white text, full pill radius. No secondary outlined or soft-fill variant.
Zero colored accent anywhere. No terracotta, blue, green, or any hue as an interactive color. CTA fill is near-black only.
Eyebrow chips use a warm soft-fill pill (no border) — not an outlined chip. Background ~#EDE9E2.
Eyebrow chips may carry schedule/status metadata (e.g. 'New patients welcome') not just category labels.
Benefits section uses the bento grid with a central photo cell. Do not default to a flat icon list or alternating-row layout.
Icon badges on bento cards are small filled circles (~36px diameter, warm fill) with stroke line-icons — not square icon blocks or colored fills.
Team/doctor cards use a tall portrait photo (full-bleed top, lg radius) with a circular social/contact badge on the card footer row.
Photography grading: warm sandy/golden, slightly desaturated, natural light. Subjects have calm neutral expressions — not broad performance smiles.
Typography is a single geometric/humanist sans at regular (400) or medium (500) weight. No serif, no script, no bold (700) headings.
Body copy is extremely spare: 1–2 sentences per block. Headline copy is 3–6 words. Whitespace communicates the calm, not words.
