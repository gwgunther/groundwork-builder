•MEET YOUR DOCTOR
Founder & Lead Dentist

Dr. Anthony Hoang, DMD

Spring St. Dentistry — Long Beach, CA

A Long Beach and Lakewood native, Dr. Hoang considers it a genuine privilege to practice dentistry in the community where he grew up. He completed his DMD at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine and his residency at the Loma Linda VA Medical Center.

What drives him every day is the human side of dentistry — the relationships he builds with patients and the trust they place in him, visit after visit. He has a particular gift for putting nervous patients at ease, creating an environment where comfort and quality care go hand in hand.

  • ✦DMD, Tufts University School of Dental Medicine
  • ✦General Practice Residency, Loma Linda VA Medical Center
  • ✦Active member, ADA · CDA · Volunteers at Long Beach Children's Dental Health Clinic
