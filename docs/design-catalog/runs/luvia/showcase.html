Novel components (3)
Bento Impact Grid + Accent Anchor Card
Asymmetric 2-col bento: tall plum accent card with service pillars + patient quote left; 2×2 grid of oversized metric-numeral cards right. Split section header (H2 left, descriptor right).
Abstract Fluid Gradient Hero + Stat Badges
Hero right half is geometric data-wellness art (metric bars + dial) in plum on cool stone. Below the copy block: icon + bold stat + muted descriptor badge row for at-a-glance social proof.
Portrait Cards with Stat Overlay
Full-bleed 3:4 portrait photo cards with gradient overlay at card base. Overlay contains a large white bold stat numeral + short descriptor label. Used for patient outcomes and team display.
Fidelity checks (12)
Hero headline is left-aligned with bold editorial serif. Right half is geometric data-wellness art — not a photograph.
Nav is embedded inside the same rounded white panel as hero content — not a separate sticky full-width bar.
Primary CTA uses saturated plum (#6B2D5B) with white text — vivid and bold, never muted terracotta-sibling to Klinik.
Bento grid includes one full-height plum card as the dominant visual anchor — not a supporting card.
Metric cards use oversized numerals (72–96px) — the numeral IS the content; descriptor is secondary small text.
Portrait photo cards have stat overlay bar baked into the card image base — not separate text below the card.
All section headers use split-row layout: eyebrow + H2 left, muted descriptor right. Never centered stacked header.
Eyebrow chips are muted pale tone with hairline border — never plum or accent-colored.
Depth achieved via surface tiers (linen → white → cream) and corner radius. Zero drop shadows.
Display headlines in key sections end with a terminal period ('Scale your smile.') — deliberate typographic signature.
Photography uses warm natural skin tones with neutral/desaturated backgrounds — no cool-tinted clinical stock.
Feature/metric card copy is maximum 2 lines. Single short descriptor per metric card — no extra copy.
