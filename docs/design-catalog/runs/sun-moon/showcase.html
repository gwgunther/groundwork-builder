About us

A Long Beach practice built on trust.

Dr. Anthony Hoang grew up in Long Beach and Lakewood. After earning his DMD from Tufts University and completing his residency at the Loma Linda VA, he returned to the community he calls home.

What drives him every day is the human side of dentistry — the relationships built visit after visit, and the trust patients place in him. He has a particular gift for putting nervous patients at ease.

Whether you're coming in for a routine cleaning or a more involved procedure, every appointment is designed around your comfort and your goals.

Novel components (4)
Filterable Timetable + Per-Row Book CTAs
Day-of-week tabs switch visible schedule rows. Each row: time, appointment type, level/category, provider avatar + name, inline Book pill. Dental: appointment availability calendar with direct booking per slot.
Image-Overlay Service Cards
Full-bleed image cards with bottom-anchored dark overlay gradient and white label + description text on top. Cards are tall portrait ratio with lg rounded corners. Hover scales image slightly.
Asymmetric Masonry Testimonials
Two-column staggered layout of white cards at varied heights. One large anchor card with patient photo + video play button. Remaining cards are text-only with display serif pull-quote headlines.
Dark Band CTA + Email Capture (Moon)
Full-width dark espresso panel — the 'moon' inversion. Left: display heading + body + inline email capture row. Right: full-bleed photo blending into dark background. Replaces separate CTA and newsletter sections simultaneously.
Fidelity checks (12)
Hero must be a split layout — text left, tall rounded image card right. Not centered. Not a poster/background-image hero.
Hero headline mixes upright and italic serif in the same line. This italic/upright combination is the signature typographic device — preserve it.
Primary button and outer chrome share the same dark espresso color (#2C2218). This visual unity is intentional — do not diverge them.
Exactly one dark-band section exists on the page (the CTA/newsletter). All other sections use cream canvas or white card. Do not add more dark backgrounds.
Dark band CTA section contains an inline email capture — not just a standalone button. Email input + Subscribe pill is the action element.
All image cards (hero, service, teacher portraits) use large rounded corners (~20–24px). No sharp or small-radius corners on image containers.
Service/class cards use image-fill with bottom-anchored text overlay gradient — not image-top-with-text-below layout.
Testimonial section uses asymmetric height staggering — not a uniform grid. At least one card is a larger video/image anchor card.
Eyebrow labels above section headings are bare all-caps micro text — no pill chips or badge containers. Location pill in hero is the one exception.
Navigation uses dot separator bullets (•) between items that have dropdown menus. Plain text for non-dropdown items. This is a distinctive nav detail.
Pricing cards use full-width pill CTAs inside each card. All cards carry equal visual weight — no 'recommended' highlighted card.
Body copy max 2–3 sentences per paragraph. Headlines are short, declarative, sentence-case. Avoid multi-clause run-on headings.
