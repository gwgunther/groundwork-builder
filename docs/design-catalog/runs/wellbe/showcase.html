Novel components (4)
Asymmetric Hero + In-Image Yellow Overlay
Large standalone ALL-CAPS headline top-left, body copy + pill CTA top-right, then full-width image card with carousel. In-image headline renders in electric yellow (#E8D800).
Program Cards: Image + ALL-CAPS Title + Price
3-column grid with full-bleed image (xl radius), ALL-CAPS ExtraBold title, description, price, and pill-outline "More" CTA per card. Section header uses inline "View All" pill-outline link.
Statement Headline on Yellow Section + Inline Form
Full-section electric-yellow background. Bold statement headline left, inline contact form right. The yellow extends wall-to-wall — not a card or banner. The one full-section accent-fill usage.
Journal Section + Embedded Newsletter Widget
Massive ALL-CAPS heading left + olive-dark signup widget card embedded top-right (avatar stack, bold heading, email input, yellow "+" submit). Article cards with yellow in-image title overlays below.
Fidelity checks (12)
All section headings must be ALL CAPS ExtraBold condensed — sentence-case headings collapse the archetype.
Hero uses asymmetric two-zone layout: standalone ALL-CAPS headline top-left, body + CTA top-right, image card below. Not centered, not background-image behind headline.
In-image headlines in hero carousel and article cards must render in electric yellow (#E8D800). Yellow is the in-image text color, not a section background by default.
Contact/CTA section uses full-section yellow background fill. Do not reduce to a card or banner — the yellow extends wall-to-wall.
Three button types must coexist: filled dark-olive primary, pill-outline transparent secondary, filled-yellow submit. Do not collapse to one type.
Image cards use xl rounded corners (16–20px). No sharp corners on any photography containers.
Category tags inside image cards are small inline ALL-CAPS plain text — not pill chips with borders.
Pricing displayed prominently below service/program titles. Not hidden or gated to detail pages.
Outer olive-green frame is structural page chrome only. Nav lives on the cream canvas, not the olive frame.
Body copy stays at 1–2 short sentences. This is a visual-dominant system — images and bold type carry the message, not paragraphs.
Newsletter widget in the journal section is an embedded olive-dark card — not a separate full-width footer banner.
Star ratings visible in all testimonial cards. Rating display is part of the compact card layout, not optional.
