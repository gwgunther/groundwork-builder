About us

Dentistry the way it should feel.

We take the time to understand your goals — and your comfort. Every visit is personal.

A Long Beach practice built on trust.

Dr. Anthony Hoang grew up in Long Beach and Lakewood. After earning his DMD from Tufts University and completing his residency at the Loma Linda VA, he returned to the community he calls home.

What drives him every day is the human side of dentistry — the relationships built visit after visit, and the trust patients place in him. He has a particular gift for putting nervous patients at ease.

Whether you're coming in for a routine cleaning or a more involved procedure, every appointment is designed around your comfort and your goals.

Fidelity checks (11)
Hero must be centered text-only — no background image. Filmstrip below, not behind.
All buttons and eyebrow chips use full pill radius. No md/lg radius on interactive elements.
Depth via warm surface-color tiers (canvas → card → tinted). Zero drop shadows.
Eyebrow chips only above primary section H1s — not on every section or card.
Accent terracotta only for primary CTAs, hover states, and metadata labels. Never decorative.
Photography must carry consistent warm ivory/linen grading. No cool-toned images.
Doctor quote block must include a cursive script signature.
Service list rows use hairline dividers and pill-outline metadata chips — not card containers per row.
Headings are sentence-case; emphasis via color shift on hover, not uppercase kickers.
Nav stays opaque white at all scroll positions. No transparent overlay.
Body copy: 2–3 sentences max per paragraph. Generous line-height and block spacing.
